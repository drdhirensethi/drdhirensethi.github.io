Geriatrics 10 Aug 2025

Acute Delirium Unmasking Polypharmacy Harm: De-prescribing Using Beers Criteria

An 81-year-old retired officer with acute delirium, identified to have nine concurrent medications including multiple Beers Criteria culprits. A structured de-prescribing exercise guided by STOPP/START and Beers Criteria resolved the delirium and improved function.

Key learning: Acute delirium in older adults demands an immediate medication reconciliation — polypharmacy is the most common and most reversible precipitant.
