Frailty through the Lens of Chronic Low-grade Inflammation in Older Adults: A Cross-sectional Study

Purohit PP, Bhasker S, Bhatia SK, Aggarwal V, Karade S, Sethi D

Journal of Indian Academy of Geriatrics 2026;22:147-52

Cross-sectional study of 387 older adults attending a tertiary care OPD, examining the prevalence of frailty and prefrailty by Fried's criteria and their association with inflammatory markers (hsCRP, IL-6) and geriatric syndromes.
